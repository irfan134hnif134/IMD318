Chapter I — About

A designer learning out loud.

My name is Hanif and I'm a final-year student at Universiti Teknologi MARA, where I study at the intersection of design, technology, and digital media. I'm drawn to work that takes its time — interfaces that feel considered, type that has been chosen rather than defaulted to, and code written like it might be read by someone you respect.

When I'm in free time, I'm probably annotating a magazine, rebuilding a personal site for the third time, or quietly photographing the moments.

Family

Where my story begins.

Raised in a supportive and caring family alongside my three brothers, I learned the values of respect, perseverance, and togetherness from an early age. Whether celebrating achievements or facing challenges, my family has always been a source of encouragement, shaping the person I am today.

Journey

Balancing responsibility and ambition.

I am the eldest son in a family of four brothers, raised in a supportive and caring environment where I learned responsibility, respect, and perseverance from an early age. These values shaped me to become a guiding figure for my younger siblings while also building my character. Now as a final-year student at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), I continue to balance family responsibilities with academic life, carrying forward the support and encouragement of my family in everything I do.

Principles

Four lines I work by.

  • 01 — Clarity is a form of respect.
  • 02 — Restraint is louder than ornament.
  • 03 — Type is the interface.
  • 04 — Ship the second draft, not the first.
