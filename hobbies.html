Chapter V — Hobbies

Things I do when the laptop closes.

No. 01

Film photography

A second-hand Pentax K1000 and a steady supply of Kodak Gold. I shoot mostly architecture and quiet street scenes around Shah Alam.

No. 02

Fishing

Mostly fishing as a way to unwind and spend time in nature. The hobby helps develop patience, concentration, and persistence while providing a rewarding and enjoyable outdoor experience.

No. 03

Badminton

playing badminton as a way to maintain an active lifestyle and challenge physically. The sport enhances focus, agility, teamwork, and determination while promoting a healthy balance between academics and personal well-being.

No. 04

Snooker

playing snooker because it combines skill, strategy, and concentration. The game helps improve focus, patience, and decision-making while providing a fun and engaging recreational activity.
